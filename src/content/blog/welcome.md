## hello, world

this is the first post on the new site. i've rebuilt my homepage more times
than i can count, and every time i told myself: *this time i'll actually
write*. so here we are.

the reason is embarrassingly simple: **write things down**. not for an
audience, not for SEO — because the act of writing is how i find out what
i actually think. a problem you can explain is a problem you mostly
understand.

## what i'll write about

the stuff worth writing is the stuff that surprised me:

- bugs that took a day to find and a minute to fix
- design decisions that looked right and turned out wrong
- small tools that made a big dent in my workflow
- numbers. real ones. how much memory, how long it took, how much it cost.

no tutorials. no hot takes. no "here's why X is the future of Y". there are
enough of those.

## what this site is

a static site, a git repo, and a CI pipeline. i write markdown, push, and
the thing deploys itself. that's the whole magic trick — the site ships
itself, i just push.

if you're reading this via rss, thanks for subscribing. there will be more.
